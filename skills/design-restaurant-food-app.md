# La Maison Restaurant Design

Use this skill to create, refine, or extend landing page content and UI layouts for the **La Maison** restaurant brand.

## Brand Concept

La Maison is an elegant fine-dining restaurant focused on award-winning cuisine, seasonal ingredients, timeless recipes, chef craftsmanship, and memorable dining experiences.

Primary message:

> Experience Culinary Art

Supporting message:

> Discover the finest flavors crafted with passion. Fresh ingredients, timeless recipes, and an unforgettable dining experience await you.

## Visual Style

Create a refined, warm, editorial restaurant website.

Style traits:

- Fine-dining restaurant landing page
- Cream background
- Dark brown typography and borders
- Bold serif headings
- Warm orange call-to-action buttons
- Elegant food photography layout
- Strong rectangular borders
- Premium but welcoming atmosphere
- Classic culinary editorial feel

## Color Palette

Use these colors consistently:

- Primary orange: `#FF6B35`
- Rich brown: `#8B4513`
- Cream background: `#FFFAF0`

Recommended usage:

- Use `#FF6B35` for primary buttons, icons, labels, prices, and highlight blocks.
- Use `#8B4513` for section headings, decorative accents, and warm luxury details.
- Use `#FFFAF0` for page backgrounds and soft content areas.

## Writing Rules

- Use elegant, sensory, refined restaurant language.
- Emphasize flavor, craft, seasonality, passion, tradition, and memorable dining.
- Keep copy premium but approachable.
- Avoid casual fast-food language.
- Avoid exaggerated health or nutrition claims.
- Use concise section copy with strong emotional appeal.
- Make CTAs direct and reservation-focused.

## Layout Rules

- Use cream backgrounds.
- Use dark brown borders and typography.
- Use strong serif headings for luxury appeal.
- Use orange buttons with bold shadows or dark outlines.
- Use editorial food imagery blocks.
- Use spacious section layouts.
- Use menu cards with dish image, name, description, and price.
- Use reservation forms with clear labels, bordered inputs, and a strong orange submit button.

## Output Expectations

When asked to create a design, provide complete section-based landing page content or implementation-ready UI copy.

When asked to generate code, preserve this brand structure, visual style, and color palette.

When asked to rewrite content, keep the La Maison fine-dining tone and avoid changing the core restaurant positioning.
